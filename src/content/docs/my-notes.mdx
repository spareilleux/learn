Every lesson has a bookmark button under its title, a bookmark button next to each section heading (it shows when you hover the heading), and a notes box at the bottom of the page. This page gathers them.

They are stored in your browser's [local storage](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Window/localStorage) and never leave it: no account, no server. Another browser or another device won't see them, and clearing this site's data deletes them. Export them to keep a copy, and import the file on another device to bring them along.

A page bookmark and a page note follow you from one language to another. A section bookmark stays in the language you added it in, because section anchors differ between languages.

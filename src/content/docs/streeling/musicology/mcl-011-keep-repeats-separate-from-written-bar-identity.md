## The project question

A learner asks an agent to revisit the second occurrence of a bar. A written measure identifier alone cannot distinguish two visits during playback. This lesson expands one bounded repeat plan while preserving stable written identity. It keeps traversal, elapsed time and notation separate, and implements no general score interpreter or performance claim.

## 1. Exercise 1

Declare written measures A, B and C, each four quarter notes long. The authored traversal is `[A,B,A,B,C]`. Written identity is unchanged when a measure returns. A visit needs a separate occurrence coordinate, otherwise later annotations can overwrite earlier observations attached to the same written bar.

How many written measures and playback visits exist?

> **Answer.** There are 3 written measures and 5 visits; B has 2 visits.

## 2. Exercise 2

Accumulating the visit durations gives starts `[0,4,8,12,16]` in quarters. The second B starts at 12, whereas the first B starts at 4. A written address such as B plus local offset is therefore incomplete for a request tied to a particular playback visit.

Where does the second B begin?

> **Answer.** At quarter coordinate 12; preserve written identity B and its second occurrence separately.

## 3. Exercise 3

The total traversal lasts 20 quarters. Deduplicating the list to `[A,B,C]` gives only 12. This negative control shows that set equality of written bars does not preserve a performed plan's ordered visits or duration. Conversely copying the written measure under a new identity is not required to represent a second visit.

What duration error follows from deduplication?

> **Answer.** The total shrinks from 20 to 12 quarters, losing 8 quarters.

## 4. Exercise 4

The primary MusicXML repeat definition distinguishes forward and backward directions and a repetition count in its stated context. Our traversal is explicitly supplied rather than derived from arbitrary repeat syntax. Alternate endings, nested sections and jumps are outside this model. Unsupported notation should remain unresolved rather than receive a plausible but untested expansion.

Can the example certify all repeat interpretation?

> **Answer.** No. Only the supplied five-visit traversal is checked; broader notation needs additional rules and tests.

## 5. Exercise 5

The retained tempo-map lesson explains how an event coordinate becomes elapsed time under a rate map. Apply that operation after selecting the intended visit and retaining its origin. A future practice annotation should identify score revision, written measure, visit, local position and tempo map. These fields support review without inventing a new mandatory schema.

What distinguishes a second-visit annotation from a written-bar annotation?

> **Answer.** A retained visit identity and traversal context; written B alone cannot identify the second occurrence.

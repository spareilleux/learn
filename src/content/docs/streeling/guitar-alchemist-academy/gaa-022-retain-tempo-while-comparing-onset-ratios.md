## Project operation and interpretation

A practice attempt preserves a rhythm’s proportions while taking longer. Comparing inter-onset intervals after division by their total removes global duration scale, so preserve that total as a separate result. Compute absolute differences of normalized intervals and their sum. This is a deliberately scale-invariant shape discrepancy, not a complete beat-clock alignment or a pass rule. It ignores the first onset’s absolute location and cannot score articulation or listening. A perfect shape result can coexist with an unacceptable tempo for a separately declared task.

## Project question

A textile dashboard averages fibre-axis labels near the wrap boundary and draws a perpendicular mean. Axes have no arrow: reversing one describes the same orientation. Position and length encodings do not fix this arithmetic mistake. This lesson derives an axial summary before displaying a direction on a proposed craft scene.

## Derive the operation before proposing automation

Doubling each axis angle turns its half-turn equivalence into a full circular turn. Average the resulting unit vectors, then halve the resultant angle. The vector length diagnoses cancellation; when it vanishes, do not draw a mean arrow. [SciPy](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.circmean.html) documents circular means and warns about the zero resultant, but this lesson declares its own null policy rather than using an implementation-defined angle. Atlas has geometric directions, without supplying a fibre survey. A single mean also hides separated orientation groups.

### Exercise 1

Compute the ordinary angle average.

<details><summary>Answer 1</summary>

It is `90` degrees, which points across the near-horizontal axes rather than along their shared orientation.

</details>

### Exercise 2

Compute the doubled-angle summary.

<details><summary>Answer 2</summary>

The resultant length is `0.9396926207859084` and the axial mean is `0` degrees. Boundary roundoff must not turn it into a different axis.

</details>

### Exercise 3

Apply the orthogonal control.

<details><summary>Answer 3</summary>

The resultant length is `0` within tolerance and the axial mean is `null`; the arithmetic mean is `45` degrees.

</details>

### Exercise 4

Does a missing mean imply missing observations?

<details><summary>Answer 4</summary>

No. The observations exist; their balanced axial directions cancel. Encode undefined summary separately from absent source data.

</details>

### Exercise 5

What should the visualization retain?

<details><summary>Answer 5</summary>

Show the raw axial marks or an orientation histogram beside the resultant length. Label the half-turn convention and avoid an arrow that implies directed flow.

</details>

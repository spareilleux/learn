## Project question

SEM-006 showed a reflected face whose unchanged indices disagreed with an outward convention. This lesson asks a different adapter question: who owns the correction when a transform stays on a node or is baked into vertices? Correcting both independently can reverse the correction twice. The case concerns orientation bookkeeping, not a claim that an exporter or Godot import works.

The glTF instantiation section relates winding to the global transform determinant. Distinguish that format rule from our baked-geometry policy: after baking a reflection, the ordered edge cross should align with the transformed outward reference. Compute the determinant of the whole linear chain, not one scale component. Two orientation reversals cancel. A cyclic permutation preserves winding; swapping a pair reverses it. Translation changes neither edge differences nor parity. A singular map needs a geometric check: determinant zero makes the inverse-normal rule unavailable, but does not mean every individual triangle necessarily collapses.

## Fixed case and assumptions

```text
A=(0,0,0); B=(2,0,0); C=(0,3,0)
(B-A) cross (C-A)=(0,0,6)
M=diag(-2,1,1); det(M)=-2
baked_cross=(0,0,-12); outward_reference=(0,0,1)
after_one_index_swap=(0,0,12)
N=diag(-1,1,1); det(M*N)=+2
Z=diag(0,1,1); triangle_cross_after_Z=(0,0,0)
```

## Corrected exercises

### Exercise 1

Calculate original cross product and area.

> **Answer:** The cross is `(0,0,6)` and area `3` square coordinate units. Cross magnitude is twice triangle area.

### Exercise 2

Bake the first transform. What operation matches the outward reference?

> **Answer:** The baked cross is `(0,0,-12)`. Swap a pair once to obtain `(0,0,12)`. This is the declared baked policy, not an instruction to duplicate a node-level correction.

### Exercise 3

Compose both reflected scales. Does the chain reverse orientation?

> **Answer:** No: the product determinant is `+2`. Blind swaps without tracking the composed representation risk a redundant correction.

### Exercise 4

Apply the zero-axis control. Can an index swap repair this triangle?

> **Answer:** Its cross becomes `(0,0,0)` and area `0`. Reordering cannot restore area. This conclusion is specific to this triangle.

### Exercise 5

What must the adapter say beyond “mirrors handled”?

> **Answer:** Name retained node transforms, baked vertex transforms, index policy, outward convention and consumer rule. State which stage corrects orientation; host visibility remains unobserved.

## The project question

Can the observatory accept every individual weave crossing and still fail to place rigid panels at globally consistent heights? The pinned [IX parallel-panel ADR](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ix/blob/d7c22fdd6a62becb66b9d7246ea85850e7c55854/docs/adr/0007-origami-thickness-as-parallel-rigid-panels.md) distinguishes a local ply reading from one height per parallel rigid panel. Its thickness is full thickness in crease-pattern units, not automatically millimetres. [SEM-008](../../semiotics/sem-008-interpret-layer-order-relative-to-the-reference-normal/) explains how a supplied layer relation becomes an interpreted above/below statement.

Here a directed edge means lower panel to upper panel. The [thickness source](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ix/blob/d7c22fdd6a62becb66b9d7246ea85850e7c55854/crates/ix-origami/src/thickness.rs#L142-L204) uses that convention after merging faces connected by flat joints. Its unit graph edge is not a physical force or a measured height.

## 1. Sum the global constraints

Use four authored panels and edges `A->B`, `B->C`, `C->D`, `D->A`. For positive full thickness `t`, require `z_upper>=z_lower+t` for every edge. Summing cancels all four height variables and yields `0>=4*t`. Positive thickness makes that impossible in this parallel-panel model.

Each crossing can still have a locally coherent lower/upper pair. The contradiction appears when those relations must share the same panel height across different crossings. These authored labels are not the face IDs of a newly executed IX fixture.

### Practice exercise 1: exhibit the global contradiction

Write the four separation inequalities and their sum for `t=0.5` crease-pattern units. Does failure of this global model mean that every individual crossing was locally contradictory?

> **Answer:** The inequalities are `z_B>=z_A+t`, `z_C>=z_B+t`, `z_D>=z_C+t` and `z_A>=z_D+t`. Their sum gives `0>=4*t`, hence `0>=2`, which is false. The cycle couples different crossings through one height per panel; a single local crossing need not contain a contradiction.

## 2. Preserve the zero-thickness boundary

At `t=0`, the same inequalities allow all heights to coincide. The directed graph still has a cycle. The pinned [weave test](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ix/blob/d7c22fdd6a62becb66b9d7246ea85850e7c55854/crates/ix-origami/tests/thickness.rs#L281-L305) expects layer analysis to pass while the rigid reading reports a cycle. Refusal is conditional on positive thickness.

For that cyclic source case, `rigid.levels` and `rigid.height` remain null even at zero thickness; the separate ply height is zero. Do not replace an absent rigid witness by an invented numeric witness. These are assertions read in test source, not fresh test results.

### Practice exercise 2: separate graph state from refusal

At zero thickness, may an adapter rewrite “not refused” as “acyclic”? What changes at positive thickness, and does either result certify manufacture?

> **Answer:** No. The graph remains cyclic; zero thickness only disables this positive-thickness rigid-stack refusal. At positive thickness the same cycle refuses that model, with no rigid level or height witness. Neither an acceptance nor a refusal certifies whether a real woven, tilted or deforming object can be made.

## 3. Remove an edge and compute levels

Delete `D->A`. The remaining chain has levels `A=0`, `B=1`, `C=2`, `D=3`. The longest directed path has three edges and four panel levels. With `t=0.5`, level offsets are `[0,0.5,1,1.5]` in crease-pattern units.

For an authored full-layer envelope convention, each panel occupies from its offset through one full thickness above it. The total extent is therefore 2 units, whereas the highest offset is 1.5. This illustrates the source report's number-of-levels times full thickness; it does not place physical panels in a native scene.

### Practice exercise 3: distinguish offsets and extent

Calculate the number of levels, highest offset and full envelope extent. Is this level assignment a unique canonical placement of the object?

> **Answer:** There are four levels, highest offset 1.5 units and full extent 2 units. A valid level witness is not a unique canonical placement: translation and other admissible arrangements can differ. The fixed abstract graph and envelope convention establish neither exact thick-fold geometry nor motion clearance.

## 4. Merge identities and retain unknown relations

Before merging, face edges `1->2` and `2->0` form an acyclic graph. If faces 0 and 1 are joined flat and must share a rigid panel, the panel map is `[0,0,2]`. The resulting panel edges are `0->2` and `2->0`: a cycle. [NET-006](../../network-science/net-006-instance-and-resource-graphs/) similarly asks which entities a graph represents; a face graph and a panel graph need not have the same constraints.

Separately, consider known edges `A->B` and `C->D`, with the overlapping pair B/C undetermined. The known graph has two levels. Completing it with `B->C` requires four, whereas `C->B` permits two. With stipulated ply two, the source-like two-level reading is only a lower bound while that pair remains unknown; exact face levels are not emitted.

### Practice exercise 4: perform both graph operations

Explain the cycle created by the flat-joint merge. For the separate unknown-pair graph at `t=0.5`, calculate the two completion extents and explain why the partial reading cannot be called exact.

> **Answer:** The merge identifies faces 0 and 1, converting the face path into the panel cycle `0->2->0`. For the unknown-pair example, the two completions require four and two levels, giving extents 2 and 1 units. The partial two-level value is a lower bound, not a decision about the missing relation. No new geometry or native checker produced these abstract examples.

## 5. Keep the graph witness with its claim

The [layer module](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ix/blob/d7c22fdd6a62becb66b9d7246ea85850e7c55854/crates/ix-origami/src/layers.rs#L92-L118) interprets supplied relations using face orientation. The thickness module then merges panels, deduplicates panel edges and retains a face-pair witness for a cycle. Unknown overlapping relations remain a separate count; an acyclic known graph does not make them known.

### Practice exercise 5: prepare a source-assisted refusal

Read the ADR, source and test. Replace “the weave is impossible” with a scoped report and specify what a future observatory adapter should preserve before proposing another craft DSL.

> **Answer:** Report that the stated relations have no positive-thickness stack with one fixed height per parallel rigid panel, and retain the cycle's lower/upper face witnesses, panel map, orientation/sign interpretation, thickness units, unknown-pair count and source revision. This says nothing universal about tilted panels, flexible yarn, folding motion or manufacturing. Compare a typed graph record and existing checker before adding a DSL. No adapter or physical trial was delivered here.

## Project question

A sculpture can have its mass projection inside its support and still slide down a sloping display. The earlier support calculation tests tipping under its own assumptions. This lesson adds a separate force inequality for a proposed Blender-to-Godot craft handoff. A stable-looking capture supplies neither contact force nor a measured friction coefficient.

## Derive the operation before proposing automation

Resolve weight into the plane and its normal. Static friction responds to the required tangential force up to its bound; it does not automatically equal that bound. A positive reserve means that this ideal inequality admits equilibrium. It does not prove that a real contact realizes the coefficient, that the support resists tipping, or that motion will stop after a disturbance. The inspected Atlas helper defines object placement and materials, without assigning these contact parameters.

### Exercise 1

Compute the normal and downhill forces.

<details><summary>Answer 1</summary>

They are `16 N` and `12 N`. Both use the same declared gravity and mass; the direction of each component matters.

</details>

### Exercise 2

Compute the bound and reserve.

<details><summary>Answer 2</summary>

The bound is `12.8 N` and the reserve is `0.8 N`. The actual static friction needed is `12 N`, below the bound.

</details>

### Exercise 3

Would doubling mass rescue a failed inequality?

<details><summary>Answer 3</summary>

No. Demand and capacity scale together, so the sign of the reserve cannot change for positive mass under these assumptions.

</details>

### Exercise 4

Apply the changed coefficient.

<details><summary>Answer 4</summary>

Capacity becomes `8 N`, reserve becomes `-4 N`, and `static_feasible=false`. The earlier tipping result cannot repair this failure.

</details>

### Exercise 5

What would justify a contact claim in the scene?

<details><summary>Answer 5</summary>

Record the slope frame, contact model, coefficient provenance and other loads. Test sliding separately from support projection and identify the engine version before comparing behavior.

</details>

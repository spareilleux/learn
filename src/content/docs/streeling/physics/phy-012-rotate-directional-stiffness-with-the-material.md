## The project question

Can rotating a woven-looking object in the observatory determine how it resists a load? [PHY-006](../../physics/phy-006-check-weave-scene-units/) establishes lengths and scene units; [PHY-008](../../physics/phy-008-keep-surface-normals-perpendicular/) establishes normal transformations. Neither supplies a material response law. This lesson adds an authored two-degree-of-freedom spring model, not a continuum fabric tensor or a calibrated textile simulation.

The [MIT linear spring lecture transcript](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-2-002-finite-element-procedures-for-solids-and-structures-spring-2010/5195e8fac694f612c5bac7515b05f6ef_oNqSzzycRhw.pdf) gives stiffness equilibrium and quadratic strain energy. Its text was read; the lecture video and PDF figures were not inspected. We use those linear relations with stipulated numbers.

## 1. Declare the response and its units

Let local displacement be `u` in mm, force be `f` in N and stiffness be `K_local=[[4,0],[0,1]]` in N/mm. Define `f=K*u` and stored energy `E=0.5*u^T*K*u` in N*mm. The two coordinates describe an ideal linear spring response around an equilibrium. They are abstract planar axes, with no prescribed Godot Euler-angle or world-height mapping.

### Practice exercise 1: compare the local axes

Find force and energy for local displacements `[1,0]` and `[0,1]`. What does the difference establish about an actual woven sample?

> **Answer:** The forces are `[4,0]` N and `[0,1]` N; energies are 2 and 0.5 N*mm. The model is direction dependent because its stipulated stiffnesses differ. These calculations establish no measured property of a physical textile.

## 2. Rotate material response, not only the silhouette

For an active mathematical rotation, define `R=[[cos(theta),-sin(theta)],[sin(theta),cos(theta)]]`. The transformed quantities are `u_world=R*u_local`, `K_world=R*K_local*R^T` and `f_world=R*f_local`. The transpose is the inverse here because the rotation is orthogonal.

At a quarter turn, `K_world=[[1,0],[0,4]]`. Identical geometry can therefore accompany different responses to the same world force. Under force `[1,0]` N, the unrotated model has displacement `[0.25,0]` mm, whereas the quarter-turned material has `[1,0]` mm. A mesh without its response law and material frame cannot identify which response was intended.

### Practice exercise 2: keep the world probe fixed

For world displacement `[1,0]` mm, compare force and energy before and after the quarter turn. Then explain the fixed-force displacement example above.

> **Answer:** Before rotation the force is `[4,0]` N and energy 2 N*mm; afterward they are `[1,0]` N and 0.5 N*mm. For fixed force `[1,0]` N, solving the two diagonal systems instead gives `[0.25,0]` and `[1,0]` mm. Fixing a force and fixing a displacement are different experiments; neither response follows from geometry alone.

## 3. Preserve coupling terms

At 45 degrees, `K_world=[[2.5,1.5],[1.5,2.5]]`. A world displacement `[1,0]` mm produces force `[2.5,1.5]` N and energy 1.25 N*mm. The transverse force is part of the rotated response. Replacing this matrix by its diagonal changes the model.

### Practice exercise 3: expose a diagonal-only substitution

For world displacement `[1,-1]` mm at 45 degrees, compute force and energy. Compare the energy obtained after silently removing the off-diagonal entries.

> **Answer:** The full matrix gives `[1,-1]` N and energy 1 N*mm. Keeping only diagonal entries gives force `[2.5,-2.5]` N and energy 2.5 N*mm. The removed terms carry coupling; their omission is not an equivalent rotation.

## 4. Distinguish coordinate covariance from a new experiment

If both displacement and response are rotated together, energy is unchanged: substituting the transformed quantities cancels the adjacent transpose/rotation factors. Force rotates with displacement. This differs from the fixed-world probes above, where the material direction changes relative to an unchanged imposed displacement.

The [authored response record](https://github.com/spareilleux/learn/blob/main/code/streeling/evidence/weave-material-2026-10-22/directional-response.json) contains 36 covariance cases across 6 angles and 6 displacements, plus separate fixed-world probes. Floating-point residues are retained alongside ideal quarter-turn and diagonal-angle examples. These are mathematical checks, not native-engine observations. The eigenvalues remain 1 and 4 N/mm, so the stipulated quadratic energy is positive for every nonzero displacement.

### Practice exercise 4: name the invariant

Why can energy stay invariant under a coordinate/material rotation while a fixed-world displacement yields a different energy? Does positive quadratic energy validate a real fabric model?

> **Answer:** Covariance rotates the displacement together with the stiffness, describing the same relative state. The fixed-world test changes their relative direction. Positive energy follows from the two positive eigenvalues in this ideal linear model; it does not validate nonlinear, slipping, buckling or irreversible behavior in real fabric.

## 5. Read the actual scene and API boundaries

The pinned [Learn teaching scene](https://github.com/spareilleux/learn/blob/eebf49cb0b69a314bd13015340335a983aa64bfe/code/streeling/weave-godot/trial.gd) makes separate boxes with colored materials and static collision bodies. [Godot rendering anisotropy](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/classes/class_basematerial3d.html#class-basematerial3d-property-anisotropy-enabled) changes a tangent-space specular highlight. [PhysicsMaterial](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/classes/class_physicsmaterial.html) describes collision properties such as friction and bounce. [StaticBody3D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/classes/class_staticbody3d.html) does not move under external forces or contacts. None of these selected controls implements the spring law above.

The pinned [IX thickness ADR](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ix/blob/d7c22fdd6a62becb66b9d7246ea85850e7c55854/docs/adr/0007-origami-thickness-as-parallel-rigid-panels.md) defines a geometric parallel-panel reading, not a measured stiffness response. A cyclic layer arrangement can pass selected layer rules while refusing a positive-thickness parallel rigid stack; that is not a universal manufacturing impossibility.

### Practice exercise 5: prepare a source-assisted material trial

Read the scene and the three Godot references. An agent proposes setting rendering anisotropy and friction to calibrate warp stiffness. Repair the claim and specify the evidence needed for a useful future comparison.

> **Answer:** Highlight shape and collision friction do not supply this directional spring law, and the current static bodies cannot demonstrate load-driven deformation. Retain specimen geometry, material directions, units, boundary conditions, applied forces and measured displacements; fit an explicitly chosen response model and reserve independent load/direction cases for checking it. Compare a simple fitted spring baseline before proposing a richer solver. Record uncertainty, failure cases and artisan review. No calibration, solver integration or physical trial occurred here.

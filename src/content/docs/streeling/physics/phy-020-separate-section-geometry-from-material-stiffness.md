## Project question

A flat metal strip and a square bar can contain the same section area while resisting bending differently. A craft agent that stores only area or a material label loses the position of material relative to the bending axis. The material-orientation prerequisite concerns constitutive direction; here the material remains isotropic and the section geometry changes.

## Derive the operation before proposing automation

For the horizontal centroidal axis, integrate squared distance over a rectangle: the section moment is width times height cubed divided by the rectangle constant in the packet. Swapping width and height changes that geometric factor even with unchanged area. Under the explicitly adopted linear curvature relation, the larger section moment gives the smaller curvature. Atlas creates rectangular prisms; the inspected helper neither solves elasticity nor certifies a fabricated strip. These inputs describe an ideal cross-section, not mesh-triangle counts.

### Exercise 1

Compute the section moments about both centroidal axes.

<details><summary>Answer 1</summary>

They are `27 mm^4` and `432 mm^4`. The fourth-power unit follows from squared distance integrated over area.

</details>

### Exercise 2

Compare the curvatures under the same moment.

<details><summary>Answer 2</summary>

They are `0.0003333333333333333 mm^-1` and `0.000020833333333333333 mm^-1`. The first is `16` times the second.

</details>

### Exercise 3

What changes in the square control?

<details><summary>Answer 3</summary>

Both section moments are `108 mm^4`; both curvatures are `0.00008333333333333333 mm^-1`, and the ratio is `1`.

</details>

### Exercise 4

Does equal area preserve bending response?

<details><summary>Answer 4</summary>

No. Both sections have area `36 mm^2`, but their distributions about the axes differ. Area alone cannot reconstruct a section moment.

</details>

### Exercise 5

Could an agent call this a metalwork safety result?

<details><summary>Answer 5</summary>

No. It would need validated material behavior, actual section measurements, boundary conditions and failure criteria. The authored linear curvature calculation supplies none of those observations.

</details>

## Prospective falsifier

The assertion that equal area and equal material imply equal directional bending curvature fails on the rectangular and square cases under the declared model.

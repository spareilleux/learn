## Project question

A Demerzel advisory result says allow, while a worker has credentials capable of changing a repository. Who authorized the effect? The pinned schema explicitly describes advisory output and prohibits mutation by that decision. Capability, recommendation and authorization are separate propositions. We model a proposed decision gate so a reviewer can reject an inference from a successful tool check to permission to act.

## Read the boundary before calculating

Read the linked prerequisite and project record before doing the arithmetic. Identify which statement comes from inspected source, which is an older recorded result, and which follows only from the premises below. A source comment, a prepared answer and an executed observation have different evidential roles. Preserve the original date and any failed or inconclusive gate when summarizing an older artifact. The direct baseline is a small handwritten calculation and a source-location table; an agent may help locate a definition, but its summary must be checked against that definition. Do not introduce a new language or automation merely to perform this finite comparison.

Assume an effect needs an applicable instruction from an authorized principal, scope matching the requested effect, and a still-valid instruction at the moment of action. The actor also needs capability, but capability cannot supply any missing authorization premise. Our finite Boolean model omits authentication, revocation races and execution; it is a reasoning aid only. Bind authority to the actual repository, effect and revision rather than a generic favorable label. A prior authorization to draft a course need not cover publication or merging. An advisor’s confidence score changes neither the principal nor the instruction’s scope.

## Corrected exercises

### Exercise 1

Evaluate the advice-only case.

> **Answer:** The result is `false`: capability and a favorable recommendation do not establish an authorized principal’s instruction.

### Exercise 2

Evaluate the wrong-scope and revoked cases.

> **Answer:** Both are `false`. Matching identity without matching effect, or an instruction no longer valid, fails the declared gate.

### Exercise 3

Which authored case permits execution in the model?

> **Answer:** Only the declared-permit case gives `true`. Its Boolean premises are fictional inputs, not evidence that a real person authorized a project mutation.

### Exercise 4

Remove capability from an otherwise permitted case. What distinction remains?

> **Answer:** Permission remains `true`, while executable ability is `false`. The model distinguishes authority from practical ability in both directions.

### Exercise 5

May the advisory allow string be copied into a mutation permission field?

> **Answer:** No. The pinned contract is advisory and mutation-prohibited. Any real effect requires its applicable authorization process and current evidence; this lesson executes no effect.

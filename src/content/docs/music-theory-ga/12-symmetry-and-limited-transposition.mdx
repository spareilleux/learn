Lesson 2 counted the modes of two symmetric scales: one for the whole-tone scale, two for the octatonic scale. This lesson asks which sets are symmetric at all. Some sets come back onto themselves under a transposition, a turn of the bracelet: Messiaen called the scales among them "modes of limited transposition". Others come back under an inversion, a mirror. [Guitar Alchemist](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ga) (GA) has mode classes for three symmetric scales, a service that recognizes Messiaen's modes, and several tests that its code calls symmetry tests. The course program finds every symmetric set among the 4,096, then checks each of GA's answers against them, with GA's own code wherever it can.

All GA links point to commit [`a826864`](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ga/tree/a826864f3a012cad88e415954bf57eca0ce12aa6). The outputs come from the command below; the entry point is `l14`.

```bash
dotnet run --project code/music-theory-ga/GaTheory -c Release -- l14
```

## Limited transposition

### The idea

Wikipedia: "Modes of limited transposition are musical modes or scales that fulfill specific criteria relating to their symmetry and the repetition of their interval groups. These scales may be transposed to all twelve notes of the chromatic scale, but at least two of these transpositions must result in the same pitch classes, thus their transpositions are "limited"" ([Mode of limited transposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mode_of_limited_transposition)). The whole-tone scale C D E F♯ G♯ A♯ moved up a whole tone gives the same notes, so it "has only 2 transpositions". The Streeling module [MUS-006 · The Scale Universe](../../streeling/music/mus-006-the-scale-universe/) says it with the bracelet: "A scale has **rotational symmetry** if rotating it by some k semitones produces the same scale. The scale's symmetry order tells you how many distinct transpositions it has."

The course counts, for each set, how many of the twelve transpositions T0 to T11 leave it unchanged ([`Lesson14.Fixing`](https://github.com/spareilleux/learn/blob/6b4039a/code/music-theory-ga/GaTheory/Lesson14.cs#L22-L27)). T0 always does. If T*a* and T*b* do, so does T*a*+*b*, so the count is always a divisor of 12, and the set has 12 divided by that count distinct transpositions. A set is limited when the count is more than 1. The program runs this over all 4,096 sets ([`Lesson14.cs#L158-L170`](https://github.com/spareilleux/learn/blob/6b4039a/code/music-theory-ga/GaTheory/Lesson14.cs#L158-L170)):

```text
== Limited transposition: the 4096 sets, by how many of the twelve transpositions map each onto itself
fixing  transpositions  sets   transposition classes
1       12              4020   335
2       6               54     9
3       4               12     3
4       3               6      2
6       2               2      1

sets with limited transpositions: 76, in 17 transposition classes
without the empty set and the chromatic scale: 15 classes, 14 set classes
```

Seventy-six sets out of 4,096. The two sets every transposition fixes are the empty set and the chromatic scale. Without them, 15 classes remain: Wikipedia's count, "If one excludes modes with 0, 1, 11 or 12 notes which can be construed as too extreme, there are 15 generalized Messiaen modes". They make 14 set classes, because one of them, the tritone scale below, is not its own mirror image: it and its inversion are two transposition classes of one set class. The next section lists them.

The steps show the symmetry: a class with 2 transpositions repeats its steps every 2 semitones, one with 6 every 6. And in every row the number of modes is the number of notes divided by the number of transpositions that fix the set: the octatonic scale has 8 notes and 4 fixing transpositions, 2 modes. Besides Messiaen's seven, the table holds what Wikipedia calls truncations, sets left when notes are removed symmetrically: the tritone, the augmented triad, the diminished seventh, "major third, tone, major third, tone", which is 4-25, "perfect fourth, semitone, perfect fourth, semitone", which is 4-9, the augmented scale, and two six-note sets that are mirror images of each other, "(Modes are "mirror" inversions of Petrushka Chord modes.)". The augmented scale, 6-20, is "arguably of musical importance", the article says, and "a truncated version of his Third Mode".

## GA's symmetric scale classes

### The idea

A mode class for a symmetric scale should have one mode per distinct rotation: one for the whole-tone scale, two for the octatonic scale ("Each octatonic scale has exactly two modes", Wikipedia's [Octatonic scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octatonic_scale)), two for the augmented scale, whose steps alternate 3 and 1. Their transpositions are 2, 3 and 4: "hexatonic collections can only be transposed four times before returning to the same group of notes again" ([Open Music Theory, "Collections"](https://viva.pressbooks.pub/openmusictheory/chapter/collections/)).

## The tritone scale

### The idea

Stravinsky's Petrushka chord is C major over F♯ major. Wikipedia: "The device uses tones that, together, make up a synthetic hexatonic scale (0 1 4 6 7 t). When enharmonically spelled C–D♭–E–G♭–G(♮)–B♭, it is called the tritone scale" ([Petrushka chord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrushka_chord); also [Hexatonic scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hexatonic_scale)). Its steps, 1 3 2 1 3 2, repeat after a tritone: 6 transpositions and 3 modes. It is one of the two 6-30 rows of the table above.

## What GA calls symmetric

### The idea

For the course, a set is symmetric under transposition when it has limited transpositions, and under inversion when it has a mirror axis, which comes in the next section. Neither depends on how the notes are written or which note comes first.

### In GA: three tests over the 4,096 sets

Besides `IsSymmetricSet`, two more tests claim to find symmetric sets:

- [`PitchClassSet.IsMonomodal`](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ga/blob/a826864f3a012cad88e415954bf57eca0ce12aa6/Common/GA.Domain.Core/Theory/Atonal/PitchClassSet.cs#L186-L189), "True if this set is the only member of its modal family (e.g. Whole Tone, Diminished)", is `FamilySize == 1`. Lessons 2 and 11 showed what a GA modal family is: the sets that contain 0 and share an interval-class vector. It feeds a dimension of GA's embeddings, "Is Symmetric/Monomodal" ([`EmbeddingSchema.cs#L881-L882`](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ga/blob/a826864f3a012cad88e415954bf57eca0ce12aa6/Common/GA.Business.ML/Embeddings/EmbeddingSchema.cs#L881-L882), [`ModalVectorService.cs#L132`](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ga/blob/a826864f3a012cad88e415954bf57eca0ce12aa6/Common/GA.Business.ML/Embeddings/Services/ModalVectorService.cs#L132)).
- [`AtonalChordAnalysisService.IsSymmetricalSet`](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ga/blob/a826864f3a012cad88e415954bf57eca0ce12aa6/Common/GA.Domain.Services/Chords/Analysis/Atonal/AtonalChordAnalysisService.cs#L217-L233), private, says "Check for equal divisions of the octave": it sorts the pitch classes and checks that the gaps between neighbors are all equal, without closing the circle. It sets the `IsSymmetrical` of GA's atonal chord analysis and adds "Symmetrical structure" to its description ([`#L201-L204`](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ga/blob/a826864f3a012cad88e415954bf57eca0ce12aa6/Common/GA.Domain.Services/Chords/Analysis/Atonal/AtonalChordAnalysisService.cs#L201-L204)). The program compiles the service as it is and calls the method by reflection ([`Lesson14.cs#L105-L109`](https://github.com/spareilleux/learn/blob/6b4039a/code/music-theory-ga/GaTheory/Lesson14.cs#L105-L109)).

The program counts what each test flags ([`Lesson14.cs#L246-L264`](https://github.com/spareilleux/learn/blob/6b4039a/code/music-theory-ga/GaTheory/Lesson14.cs#L246-L264)):

## Mirror symmetry

### The idea

A set can also come back onto itself under an inversion I*n*, which sends pitch class *p* to *n* − *p* (lesson 4). On the bracelet, I*n* is a mirror across the line through *n*/2 and *n*/2 + 6. Ian Ring lists these lines as "Reflection Axes": `[0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5]` for [the whole-tone scale](https://ianring.com/musictheory/scales/1365), `[1, 2.5, 4, 5.5]` for [the octatonic scale 2925](https://ianring.com/musictheory/scales/2925), `[0.5, 2.5, 4.5]` for [the augmented scale 819](https://ianring.com/musictheory/scales/819). The course counts the inversions that leave the set unchanged ([`Lesson14.Mirrors`](https://github.com/spareilleux/learn/blob/6b4039a/code/music-theory-ga/GaTheory/Lesson14.cs#L32-L34)), and draws them:

<BraceletMirrors role="img" aria-label="Three bracelets with their mirror axes dashed. 1365, the whole-tone scale, 0 2 4 6 8 10: six axes. 2925, the octatonic scale, 0 2 3 5 6 8 9 11: four axes. 2457, GA's augmented scale, 0 3 4 7 8 11: three axes." />

Six, four and three: as many axes as transpositions that fix each scale. That is no accident. Two mirrors in a row make a transposition, so if a set has one axis, each transposition that fixes it turns that axis into another one. A set has either no axis or as many as its fixing transpositions.

## Key takeaways

- A set has limited transpositions when a transposition other than T0 maps it onto itself. The number of such transpositions divides 12; the set has 12 divided by it distinct transpositions, and its number of notes divided by it distinct modes. Seventy-six sets qualify: 15 classes, Messiaen's seven modes and eight truncations, among them the augmented scale and the tritone scale.
- A set with a mirror axis has exactly as many axes as fixing transpositions. Thirteen of the 15 classes have them; the tritone scale does not, so it and its mirror image are two classes.
- GA's `IsSymmetricSet` and `IsInversionallySymmetric` agree with the course on every set. `IdentifyMessiaenMode` compares prime forms with ids that are not prime forms, and finds only modes 1 to 3; its tests accept no answer.
- GA's three symmetric mode classes have the right transposition counts but list a mode per degree, 6, 8 and 6, for 1, 2 and 2 distinct modes; two diminished names are swapped. `Scale.Tritone` has A where the tritone scale has B♭, which breaks its symmetry.
- `IsMonomodal`, the atonal file's `IsSymmetric` and `IsSymmetricalSet` are not symmetry tests: they count single members or equal gaps. The agent's answer to "modes of limited transposition" lists a single note and misses the octatonic scale, with Forte numbers its own catalog says must not be shown.

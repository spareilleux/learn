*One minute of the cinematic, from late afternoon to night and back toward dawn: 62 s, 9.3 MB, with sound.*

The [Observatory](../) is a Godot scene. To see whether another engine could serve it, a prototype was built with [BRUTAL](https://github.com/ahwootech/BRUTAL), Ahwoo's C# framework over Vulkan. It is an evaluation, not a move away from Godot, and there is nothing to download.

## What the video shows

- **The swarm:** ten thousand bodies, simulated by Jolt Physics at 120 steps a second, fly between the towers and gather into a colossal face. Its eyes and mouth glow.
- **The city:** twelve brutalist tower variants, modelled by rule in Blender, with ribbon windows, egg-crate grids, fins and warning lamps.
- **The blocks:** a structure on a mesa, generated by a small F# library from typed, nested blocks.
- **The light:** each pixel follows one path of up to three bounces, with a shadow ray toward the sun at each. DLSS Ray Reconstruction denoises the paths, and DLSS Quality upscales 1067 × 600 to 1600 × 900.
- **The night:** the sun sets on a 128-second cycle. The sky fills with stars, and the windows and lamps light up.
- **The sound:** the swarm, the wind, a wordless chant and rushes, all synthesised in C# when the program starts, and placed in the scene with OpenAL Soft. There is no voice.

## How it was measured

The video was rendered on one machine, a GeForce RTX 5080, on 2026-10-21. The program records at a fixed 60 frames a second, whatever its pace, and blends each pair into one frame of a 30 fps video: 6,720 frames for 112 seconds, of which this is one minute. The GPU took 7.37 ms a frame at the median and 13.34 ms at the 95th percentile, for the path tracing, the denoising and the upscaling together.

## Why there is no download

- BRUTAL's licence, MIT-based, asks every distributed build to credit Ahwoo, to show the unaltered BRUTAL logo before it starts, and to ship its `NOTICE.txt`. This prototype is a local experiment and is not distributed.
- DLSS needs NVIDIA's Streamline libraries, which are not redistributed here.

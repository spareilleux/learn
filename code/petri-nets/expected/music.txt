The place invariant is the conservation of voices: no move creates or drops one,
so every reachable marking is a three-note chord, and nothing else can be.
The transition invariants are the closed voice leadings: the chromatic scale
all the way round in either direction, and every move followed by its undo.

== Widening the moves to a tone breaks the transition invariants, not the place one ==
places: 12   transitions: 48   rank of C: 11
place invariant   C + Cs + D + Ds + E + F + Fs + G + Gs + A + As + B = 3
transition invariants: the solutions of C x = 0 form a cone of dimension 37 in 48 unknowns, and Farkas' elimination does not finish on it.
Place invariants stay cheap because they live in one dimension per place.

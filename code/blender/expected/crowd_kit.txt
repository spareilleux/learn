
== A vertex's place cannot tell an arm from a leg
leg top, outer edge from the middle: 0.170 m
arm at the shoulder, inner edge from the middle: 0.145 m
overlap: 0.025 m, so the UVs flag them: |u| 1 or 0.6 a leg, |u| 2 an arm

## Course production

1. GAS007 derives the pinned body's two-pole recurrence, distinguishes pole angle from gain maximum, combines responses as complex quantities and separates a steady LTI response from a startup transient and physical calibration. Existing GAS005/006 and AUD007 are prerequisites rather than repeated lessons.
2. AUD008 uses a declared periodic two-tap experiment to compare paired complex input/output transforms. It includes output-only confounding, undefined unexcited bins, weak excitation and a finite linear convolution cropped before its tail. It builds on AUD006 amplitude calibration and CS013 axes.
3. INF008 constructs unequal reversed two-tap impulses with equal magnitudes, demonstrates phase and temporal-descriptor differences, and distinguishes Fourier input domains in the actual GA paths. It does not generalize to every constrained overlapping spectrogram inversion.

## Limits and next evidence

The strongest objection to treating this model as a physical guitar is that an output bank is not a measured, coupled bridge/radiation model. A simpler next step is an isolated analytic transfer check before any engine or orchestration extension. Reject a physical-accuracy claim without defined measurement geometry, calibrated paired data and held-out criteria. Reject a transfer estimate without adequate excitation and a justified boundary/noise model. Reject waveform identity inferred from magnitude equality alone. No perceptual improvement or learner success may be inferred from these mathematical checks.

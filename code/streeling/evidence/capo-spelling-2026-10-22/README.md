# Capo coordinates, spelled transposition and degree context

This packet supports GTR008, MCL008 and WML007 in English, French and Spanish. Canonical course sources live in `streeling/courses/`; existing synchronization generates site pages.

## Input boundaries

- `primary-readback.json` binds six anonymous GA downloads to immutable local Git objects at the named revision. A working-tree snippet and its checkout HEAD are not a byte-level source binding.
- `theory-tool-probes.json` retains three actual read-only chord-transposition responses with unknown service revision. They are not CapoSkill execution, native parser verification, a capo/fret adapter or playing evidence.
- `capo-coordinates.json` contains eight authored full-capo cases and separate invalid/muted and partial-capo controls in ideal twelve-tone equal temperament. No measured intonation or playability follows.
- `spelled-transpositions.json` contains ten authored two-coordinate transformations and inverses. No new MusicXML document, parser, rendered score or playback was produced.
- `degree-context.json` contains six stipulated tonic/form contexts, bounded degree rows and explicit vocabulary controls. It is not a universal tradition classifier, key detector or syllable service.
- `source-observations.json` records actual selected-source scope. `primary-reading.json` records successful primary text reading and failed unused attempts separately. Earlier courses retain their own revision boundaries.
- `data-audit.json` and `content-audit.json` provide independent AI checks with exact input hashes. They do not supply human, musician, fluent-language or learner acceptance.
- `reviewer-rubric.json` and `learner-task.md` prepare a future actual attempt; all learner decisions remain null until submitted answers and assistance are retained and reviewed.
- `amended-locale-checks.json` proves a surgical GTR001 correction in all three languages: one-semitone B/C and E/F spacing does not prohibit B# or E# spelling. The remaining old course and its metadata are byte-preserved. The content audit reviews these three changed passages separately from the 45 new localized answer pairs; it does not claim a full review of the old course.

## Publication and review

Validation records existing generator tests, synchronization, static build, changed article/anchor/source-banner checks and repeat-sync equality. External transport success is distinct from actual document reading. A final immutable public source readback is retained outside the repository after push to avoid circular commit identification. Source readback does not establish deployed course HTML.

Human technical, musician, fluent-language and actual learner reviews remain pending. No production adapter, parser, new DSL, application import, instrument trial or learning effect is delivered. The goal remains 525 reviewed and published courses across the original 21 departments; source inventory measures only one part of that goal.

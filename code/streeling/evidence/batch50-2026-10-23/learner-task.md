Status: prepared, not performed. Use each course's source-assisted task and its five prepared criteria in the corresponding unit packet. Work in the course language and retain original answers before feedback. Do not substitute expected answers or an AI self-check for a participant attempt.

For each attempt retain:

- Course ID, source revision, language, date and actual learner identity or agreed pseudonym.
- Actual source files/locations consulted, calculations, stated assumptions, units and the direct/manual baseline.
- Answer to each of the five criteria, including missing or refused answers, negative-control result and a condition that would falsify the proposed conclusion.
- Tools, hints, model output, assistance and later corrections, separately from the original answer.
- Elapsed work only when actually measured; no invented time or confidence score.
- Reviewer identity, criterion decisions, reasons and unresolved questions.

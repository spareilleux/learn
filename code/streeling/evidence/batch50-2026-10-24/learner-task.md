# Source-assisted learner record

The five criteria per course are prepared acceptance targets. Neither author calculations nor AI review constitute an actual learner attempt. Human technical, language, craft/musician and learner decisions remain pending until their own evidence exists.

## Actual task

Choose one assigned lesson and retain the exact course/source revision. Before revealing its corrected answers or asking an assistant, record the project question, units, assumptions, your calculation and your conclusion about the negative control. Use the actual project/prerequisite passage named by that course and retain its path/revision and relevant lines. The direct baseline is your own table, calculation, diagram or small manual trace; an assistant-generated answer is a separate assisted attempt.

After that first record, read the corrected answers and use tools or assistance as needed. Keep the original answer, each assistance step and your corrected answer. Explain what changed and whether the course's claim survives its falsifier. If the prompt, source or answer is wrong or ambiguous, record the defect and a proposed check rather than silently adapting the question to match an answer.

## Submission fields

- Course ID, source revision, date and task chosen.
- Actual learner identity or agreed anonymous identifier.
- Exact source/prerequisite passage and how it bears on the project problem.
- Original answer and intermediate values before assistance.
- Model and control verdicts with units and assumptions.
- Assistance history, including prompts, tools and answer disclosure.
- Corrected answer, reason for each change and remaining uncertainty.
- Evidence addressing each of the course's five prepared criteria.
- Authorized reviewer's criterion decisions and overall learner decision, initially undecided.

A missing answer, missing assistance history or unanswered criterion remains unknown. Do not fill it with the authored expected result. Do not infer skill acquisition, transfer, live software improvement or user-study effects from the production test suite. A practical scene/audio/material trial needs a separately recorded actual environment, inputs, observation and comparison.

## Refinement loop

Add the result to the dated journal without overwriting the earlier attempt. Diagnose whether an error belongs to the lesson, the checker, source interpretation, the environment or the attempted solution. Correct the relevant artifact, add a useful regression check, replay it, and renew affected review bindings before publication. A rejected hypothesis or failed gate remains part of the record.

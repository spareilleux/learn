# Retry and admission teaching evidence

NET007, CYB006 and PHI005 examine dependency readiness, retry workload and claims about repeated effects. Each has five corrected exercises in English, French and Spanish. The deliverable is course prose and fixed evidence, not a new scheduler, effect store, schema or runnable lab.

## Sources, observations and models

- `production-plan.md` records predictions before probes and the subsequent correction of an overstrong parallel-executor description.
- `primary-readback.json` binds five public IX files at commit 456df065ee837e33b5bbe22a4df0616a4fcb76e5, anonymously retrieved and matched to immutable local Git objects. The dirty IX checkout was not edited. Semantic search required login; source discovery used a bounded fallback.
- `topology-tool-probes.json` preserves three actual responses, including a cycle error. Level widths describe graph structure, not observed worker counts.
- `execution-tool-probes.json` preserves two identical three-step statistics requests and one deliberate missing-tool error. Numeric maps agree while sibling orders differ. Cache hits are empty and keys null; the schema's absent asset-name input was not bypassed. Reported 0 ms is not zero cost. Service revision remains unknown.
- `observed-probe-summary.json` derives bounded comparisons while retaining raw receipts separately. The failing response has no final partial-results packet; earlier-step execution is not observed by this packet alone.
- `admission-model.json` constructs zero-overhead schedules. The actual inspected executor's map/collect directly computes synchronously despite parallel-thread comments; source names and readiness do not prove actual concurrency. No Rust execution was run.
- `retry-load-model.json` enumerates eight equally likely pre-sampled strings under independent failure probability 1/2, stopping at first success and at most 3 attempts. Expected attempts 7/4 and success probability 7/8 are assumption-bound. Its fluid example measures mean offered attempt-work, not a sampled queue or production capacity.
- `delayed-completion-model.json` constructs two traces with three-tick attempts. A two-slot cap stops a third admission, not the already active second attempt.
- `effect-identity-model.json` supplies pure state-transition and ideal serial atomic-deduplication counterexamples. It does not implement concurrent durable deduplication, crash recovery or external-effect reconciliation.

## Review and publication boundaries

The data audit retains resolved Unicode-decoding and source-comment/control-flow findings. Independent data/content review, all 45 localized exercise-answer checks, 15 prepared learner criteria, locale parity, generator tests, build, local article link/anchor readback, repeat-sync equality and source inventory are recorded separately. Public immutable source-byte readback is kept in an external receipt to avoid circular hashes.

Human technical approval, fluent-language approval, learner attempts, actual worker concurrency, queue/retry-controller tests, cache-hit testing, durable/concurrent effect-store tests, crash recovery and deployed course HTML remain unperformed. No NL model compiler, paid provider, dependency installation, heavy Rust build, GPU/audio or real external mutation was invoked. An independently reviewed source draft is not complete delivery of a reviewed-and-deployed course under the 525-course objective.

# From a voicing proposal to a bounded learning claim

This packet supports GAA007, COG007 and PHI007. It contains authored keys, attempt outcomes, logical countermodels and exact finite calculations. It does not contain a new quiz program, learning controller, real participant record, recruitment, physical performance or causal study.

## Packet contents

- `production-plan.md` states the predictions and gates before calculation.
- `primary-readback.json` binds six anonymously downloaded GA source/test files at `76efbffd5af82b46c3146957a6bd4504008eeb89`, exactly equal to immutable local Git objects. The live primary GA worktree differs from the selected revision and was not treated as that revision's source. No source file was edited.
- `primary-reading.json` records the actual APS2006 publisher abstract and NIST randomized-design web body. The author-hosted PDF web attempt timed out and was not claimed read. No study effect size, participant data or generic sample-size recommendation is imported.
- `practice-items.json` distinguishes high-to-low raw versus low-to-high displayed diagrams, four authored candidates and target-specific cues. Under the declared standard MIDI tuning, exact-A and restricted any-C-major-triad acceptance differ. A symbolic accepted answer is not a player result. Retrieval score clamps do not calibrate delayed learner recall.
- `recall-trace.json` follows one invented learner through eight practice offers and four retries: twelve offer/attempt rows, eleven completed responses. Raw first3/7, eligible observed first2/6, pooled6/11, final observed6/7 and hinted-retry3/3 describe different endpoints. Missing eligible practice and delayed responses remain explicit; deterministic completion bounds are not confidence intervals or imputations disguised as observations.
- `counterfactual-gains.json` concerns a separate population of eight fictional learners. It constructs three worlds agreeing on pretest4/8 and treated posttest6/8, but having average effects1/4,0,-1/4. All256binary no-treatment posttest vectors yield tight finite-population bounds-1/4to3/4 without an assumed prior. Each world's70hypothetical balanced learner allocations produce210exact contrasts in total; assignment averages equal the fixed effects while individual contrasts can differ in sign. Nothing was randomized in a real study.
- `source-observations.json` keeps voicing payloads, target/cue requirements, practice-plan/tracking labels and retrieval metrics separate from observed answers and learning outcomes. Selected test assertions are not a fresh test run; selected source boundaries are not a repository-wide absence proof.
- `data-audit.json` independently recomputes notes, denominators, missingness and counterfactual/allocation arithmetic and binds source provenance. `content-audit.json` records independent AI lesson review once complete.
- `learner-task.md` and `reviewer-rubric.json` prepare transfer work. Their prepared criteria do not represent performed learner decisions.

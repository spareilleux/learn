# A Minimum Distance Does Not Identify the Voices

## Project question and deliverable

A GA agent has a minimum distance. Can it tell a guitarist which line each note follows? Produce two explicit correspondence tables, map them onto two guitar strings, and identify what the score cannot decide. Read [MUS-015](mus-015-choose-voice-leading-equivalences.md) first: a distance depends on the equivalences chosen.

The pinned [GA distance method](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ga/blob/dc2e74cbd992a298f67efb928b5b3754615e1924/Common/GA.Domain.Services/Atonal/VoiceLeadingSpace.cs) returns a scalar. It does not return an assignment, a signed motion, a string choice or a duration. The [teaching lab](https://github.com/spareilleux/learn/tree/codex/streeling-craft-transfer-20261022/code/streeling/voice-leading) independently enumerates assignments for small fixed cases and retains selected witnesses. Those witnesses are new teaching output, not an existing GA API feature.

## 1. Recover two equally minimal assignments

Take source `[60,62]` and target collection `[64,65]`. Enable octave and permutation equivalence; disable transposition and inversion. The native scalar is 7. Exhaustive enumeration finds two assignments with that same cost:

| Plan | First labeled voice | Second labeled voice | Signed motions | Total circular cost |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| A | 60 → 64 | 62 → 65 | +4, +3 | 7 |
| B | 60 → 65 | 62 → 64 | +5, +2 | 7 |

Both voice motions are less than half an octave, so each circular pair cost here equals its absolute registered motion. That equality is specific to these pairs; octave-equivalent cost can hide an octave elsewhere.

### Practice exercise 1

Does a distance of 7 uniquely select plan A? Explain using both sums.

> **Answer:** No. Plan A costs 4 + 3 = 7 and plan B costs 5 + 2 = 7. The scalar discards which correspondence produced it. A tie-breaking rule could choose one, but that would be an additional policy.

## 2. Separate a voice label from pitch order

Initially the first voice is lower: 60 is below 62. In plan A it remains lower, since 64 is below 65. In plan B the first labeled voice ends above the second, since 65 is above 64. Relative pitch order reverses. Calling the lowest target entry “the first voice” after sorting would silently relabel plan B.

This is a discrete correspondence example. We have specified neither note durations nor continuous pitch trajectories. It does not establish an audible crossing during a recorded performance. Likewise, a low total motion does not by itself establish stylistic acceptability or the absence of parallel intervals.

### Practice exercise 2

For a task that requires labeled voices to preserve their relative pitch order at the endpoints, which plan meets that requirement?

> **Answer:** Plan A. Plan B reverses the endpoint order even though its total cost is also 7. State the order requirement explicitly; it cannot be recovered from the minimum alone.

## 3. Put the same notes on a guitar

The [pinned default tuning](https://github.com/GuitarAlchemist/ga/blob/dc2e74cbd992a298f67efb928b5b3754615e1924/Common/GA.Domain.Core/Instruments/Tuning.cs) includes open G3 = 55 and B3 = 59 in this numeric convention. Adding one fret adds one semitone. Use string names to retain identity:

| State | G string | B string | Resulting labeled pitches |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source | Fret 5 | Fret 3 | 60, 62 |
| Plan A target | Fret 9 | Fret 6 | 64, 65 |
| Plan B target | Fret 10 | Fret 5 | 65, 64 |

These are constructed placements, checked by addition. They were not generated by a GA fingering solver or played by a participant. A voice staying on one string is one possible identity rule; a musical line can also change strings. String identity and musical voice identity must therefore be declared separately.

### Practice exercise 3

Verify the two notes of plan B from the open-string pitches. Does the pitch-order reversal require the strings to exchange their physical positions?

> **Answer:** 55 + 10 = 65 and 59 + 5 = 64. No. The G string remains the G string and the B string remains the B string; fretting can reverse the relative pitches they sound. No physical string crossing is claimed.

## 4. Inspect a witness and its evidence boundary

In the [retained native results](https://github.com/spareilleux/learn/blob/codex/streeling-craft-transfer-20261022/code/streeling/evidence/voice-leading-2026-10-22/native-results.json), find `ambiguous_plan`. It records `minimumAssignmentCount` as 2 and retains the two `witnesses`. Their `motions` are registered target-minus-source differences; `voiceCosts` are absolute or octave-circular costs according to the case settings. Read these fields separately.

To rerun, follow the installed [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/api/cli.html) and [.NET](https://learn.microsoft.com/dotnet/core/tools/dotnet-build) instructions in MUS-015. The standalone run executes unchanged source in a separate assembly with a disclosed annotation adapter. Windows is assessed; Linux / WSL and macOS remain to verify. It does not execute the guitar placement table through a solver.

### Practice exercise 4

For the separate counterexample `[60,63,65]` matched to `[60,75,65]`, why can `motions` contain 12 while every circular `voiceCosts` entry is 0?

> **Answer:** The second labeled pitch rises from 63 to 75, a registered motion of 12 semitones. Octave equivalence maps that pair to circular cost 0. The witness preserves the registered difference; the chosen objective ignores its octave component.

## 5. Write a performance-facing handoff

A useful handoff gives the pitch collection, selected correspondence, identity rule, tie policy and remaining playing questions. For this exercise, select plan A if preserving endpoint pitch order is required. Leave finger choice, comfort and articulation unassessed until a player tests a concrete placement. The [review packet](https://github.com/spareilleux/learn/blob/codex/streeling-craft-transfer-20261022/code/streeling/evidence/voice-leading-2026-10-22/learner-task.md) gives a task that can be checked from the evidence without inventing a listening result.

### Practice exercise 5

An agent writes “distance 7, therefore the easiest fingering.” Replace that claim with a defensible statement.

> **Answer:** “Both declared assignments have cost 7 under the stated equivalences. We select plan A to preserve endpoint pitch order. Its G-string fret 9 and B-string fret 6 placement is constructed; comfort and finger choice still require a playing trial.” Continue with [ranking candidates](mus-017-audit-voice-leading-rankings.md).
